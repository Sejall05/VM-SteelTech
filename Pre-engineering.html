PRE ENGINEERED BUILDINGS

is a new manufacturing concept.

There has been a substantial rise in Pre Engineered Buildings in recent years in India due to rapid construction developments and an increase in the need for quick-track construction. PEBs is seen as a breakthrough in the construction industry, resulting in less construction time, cost-effectiveness, high quality, low maintenance, and consistent span. Pre-engineered buildings meet one of industry’s most critical criteria: wide, column-free areas. In addition to being economically efficient, pre-engineered buildings are environment-friendly ways to counter global warming.

Secondary Framing:

Structural members are the Purlins, Girts, Eave Struts and diagonal/frame/angle bracings. Purlins support the roofing while Girts support the side wall cladding. Purlins and Girts also help in resisting part of longitudinal forces generated due to wind or earthquake loads and provide lateral bracing to the compression flanges of the main frame members, thereby improving strength.
